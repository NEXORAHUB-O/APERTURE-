Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 19, 2026

Welcome to Aperture. We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about the information that may be processed when you use our website and services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected or processed, how it may be used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information You Provide

Depending on the features available on Aperture, you may voluntarily provide information such as your email address, account information, or other information that you submit while using the website.

You should avoid submitting sensitive personal information unless a particular Aperture feature specifically requires it.

2. Account and Authentication Information

Aperture may use Supabase to provide account authentication and related services.

If you create or use an Aperture account, information associated with your account may be processed by the authentication system. Depending on the authentication method used, this may include information such as your email address, authentication identifiers, account status, and authentication-related information.

Authentication systems may also record security and authentication events such as account creation, sign-in, email verification, password changes, password recovery, token refreshes, and sign-outs.

3. Information You Submit to Aperture

Some Aperture features may allow you to enter information into forms, tools, or other interactive features.

Information submitted through such features may be processed or stored when necessary to provide the requested functionality.

The specific information stored depends on the feature being used and how that feature is implemented.

4. Technical and Usage Information

When Aperture is accessed, technical information may be processed by the website, hosting infrastructure, security services, or backend services used by Aperture.

This may include information such as IP address or related network information, browser or user-agent information, request information, device or environment information, timestamps, and technical information relating to errors or service usage.

Some of this information may be generated automatically by the infrastructure used to operate and secure Aperture.

5. How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes including:

  • Providing and operating Aperture and its features.
  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Authenticating users and maintaining secure sessions.
  • Protecting the website against abuse and unauthorized access.
  • Detecting and investigating technical problems.
  • Improving the reliability and functionality of the service.
  • Responding to questions, feedback, and support requests.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

6. Supabase

Aperture uses Supabase for backend services, which may include authentication, database functionality, and other infrastructure required by the website.

Information processed through these services may therefore be stored or processed using Supabase infrastructure in accordance with the configuration and functionality of Aperture.

Supabase may also generate technical and security logs associated with requests and authentication activity.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

Aperture or services used by Aperture may use cookies, local storage, session storage, or similar browser technologies when necessary for authentication, security, functionality, or other website operations.

The availability and use of these technologies may depend on the features you use and your browser settings.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through Aperture against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or loss.

However, no internet transmission or online storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant service, maintain account functionality, protect the website, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or fulfill other legitimate operational purposes.

Actual retention may vary depending on the type of information, the feature involved, and the services used to process it.

10. Third-Party Services and Links

Aperture may use third-party services to provide hosting, backend functionality, authentication, security, analytics, or other features.

Aperture may also contain links to third-party websites. We do not control those websites and are not responsible for their privacy practices, content, security, or policies.

11. Information We Do Not Intentionally Request

Unless a specific feature clearly requires it, Aperture does not intentionally request information such as government identification numbers, passwords belonging to other services, financial account credentials, or highly sensitive personal information.

Please do not submit sensitive information to Aperture through features that are not specifically designed to receive it.

12. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law and the information involved, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or information about personal data associated with your use of Aperture.

If you have a privacy-related request, please contact us using the contact information provided on our Contact page.

13. Children's Privacy

Aperture does not intentionally seek to collect personal information from children through features that are not designed for such collection.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to Aperture, please contact us so that the situation can be reviewed.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Aperture, our services, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page and the "Last Updated" date will be changed accordingly.

15. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the way information is handled by Aperture, please visit our Contact page.
